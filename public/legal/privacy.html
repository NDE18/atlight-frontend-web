6. Sharing Your Data

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your information only in the following cases:

  • Other users: information on your public profile (name, photo, sport, club, etc.) is visible to other Atlight members.
  • Service providers: hosting and infrastructure providers acting under contract with confidentiality guarantees.
  • Legal obligations: when required by law or a competent authority.

Your private messages are accessible only to the participants of the conversation.

7. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on our secured servers. On your device, the authentication token and certain session information are stored using the system's secure storage (flutter_secure_storage), protected by the Android/iOS keystore.

All communications between the App and our servers are encrypted (HTTPS / WSS).

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or destruction.

8. Data Retention

  • Active account data: retained for as long as the account is active.
  • After account deletion: data is deleted or anonymized within 30 days, unless a legal retention obligation applies.
  • Technical logs: retained for a maximum of 12 months.

9. Minimum Age & Children's Privacy

The Atlight App is intended for users 13 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we learn that a user is under 13, we will delete their account and associated data promptly. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data without your consent, please contact us at kongnenderodrigue@gmail.com.
